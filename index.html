Music Identity

Every serious release should have the right codes behind it.

Without proper identifiers, songs can be misclassified, royalties can be delayed, and ownership records can get messy. Kay Real Life helps artists understand the foundation behind professional music registration.

✔ Better royalty tracking ✔ Professional metadata ✔ Global identification ✔ Ownership clarity

🎵 ISRC

Identifies a specific sound recording and helps track streams and royalties.

📦 UPC

Identifies a release like an album or single across digital stores.

🆔 ISNI

Separates artists with similar names and improves metadata accuracy.

✍️ IPI

Helps identify songwriters and publishers for publishing royalty collection.

🎤 IPN

Supports performer identification and royalty administration.

📝 ISWC

Identifies the composition itself and helps track songwriter royalties.
