Why Publishing Matters

Music is creative art, but it is also intellectual property.

Publishing helps creators protect ownership, organize rights, receive proper credit, and unlock revenue opportunities from their music worldwide.

📖

Music Publishing

Publishing covers the composition itself, including lyrics, melody, and songwriting ownership. It ensures the creators behind the song are properly recognized.

🛡️

Ownership Protection

Clear ownership records help prevent disputes and establish who controls, administers, and benefits from the music.

📋

Rights Management

Proper rights management keeps information organized across platforms, collection societies, publishers, and industry databases.

🌍

Global Recognition

Correct publishing data helps songs be identified worldwide, making it easier to track usage and collect royalties across different territories.

Industry Registrations

The codes and registrations that help your music get recognized.

Professional music releases rely on industry identifiers that help platforms, organizations, and rights systems correctly identify creators and recordings.

🎵

ISRC

A unique identifier assigned to every sound recording, helping platforms track and report music usage.

💿

UPC

A product code used to identify albums, EPs, singles, and music releases across stores.

✍️

IPI

Identifies songwriters, composers, and publishers within royalty collection systems.

🆔

ISNI

An international identifier that helps distinguish creators from others with similar names.

🏛️

PRO Membership

Joining performance rights organizations helps creators collect royalties from public performances.

🌍

Music Databases

Registrations with global music databases improve discoverability and industry recognition.
