Our Story

Built by creators who understand the journey of independent artists.

KAY REAL LIFE is a music distribution, artist services, and digital media company dedicated to helping independent artists, songwriters, producers, DJs, and content creators grow their careers and reach global audiences.

Founded in August 2018 by Kato Ramathan, Kitimbo Hakim, and Kitimbo Muhamad Wasswa (DJ Smithkline), KAY REAL LIFE was created with a mission to provide affordable, professional, and accessible music industry services to creators in Uganda and around the world.

Our services include music distribution to major streaming platforms, artist support and guidance, content monetization assistance, music publishing support, digital marketing, music promotion, website development, and industry registration assistance.

We also help creators understand and access important music industry systems and identifiers, enabling them to better manage, protect, and monetize their creative works while maintaining ownership and control of their content.

Through our growing ecosystem, we aim to empower artists with the tools, knowledge, and opportunities needed to build sustainable careers and share their talent with the world.
