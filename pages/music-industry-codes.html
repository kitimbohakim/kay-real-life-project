Core Industry Codes

The identifiers every serious creator should understand.

These codes help recordings, releases, songwriters, performers, and publishers stay connected to the global music ecosystem.

🎵 ISRC

International Standard Recording Code

Identifies a specific sound recording and helps track streams, sales, reporting, and royalties.

💿 UPC

Universal Product Code

Identifies an album, EP, or single release across digital stores and music platforms.

🆔 ISNI

International Standard Name Identifier

Distinguishes creators from others with similar names and improves metadata accuracy.

✍️ IPI

Interested Party Information

Used to identify songwriters, composers, and publishers within royalty systems.

🎤 IPN

International Performer Number

Helps identify performers and supports performer royalty administration.

📖 ISWC

International Standard Musical Work Code

Identifies the composition itself and helps track songwriter and publishing royalties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about music industry codes.

Understanding these identifiers can help creators release music more professionally and avoid confusion when dealing with platforms and rights systems.

What is an ISRC code?

An ISRC identifies a specific sound recording and helps track streams, sales, and royalty reporting.

Do I need a new ISRC for every song?

Yes. Every unique recording should have its own ISRC to ensure accurate identification and tracking.

What is the difference between ISRC and UPC?

ISRC identifies a recording, while UPC identifies the release package such as a single, EP, or album.

What is an ISWC?

ISWC identifies the musical composition itself and is commonly associated with songwriter royalties.

Can I release music without these codes?

Some distributors may assign certain identifiers, but understanding and managing them professionally is highly recommended.

Who provides music industry codes?

Different organizations provide different codes, including distributors, publishers, collection societies, and registration agencies.
